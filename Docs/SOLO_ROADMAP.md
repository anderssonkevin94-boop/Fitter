# Solo play roadmap (agreed 2026-10-11)

The supplier is the only character working the company. Everything below was agreed in the brainstorm. Build order:

1. **Solo play (done 2026-10-11).**
   - No hired crew.
   - The supplier mines (shaft and tunnel faces) and chops (the current clearing's trees) by holding the action button. Gathered units go straight into the carry, or wait at the face or stump when the carry is full.
   - Routines: leave yourself on one job (mine a face, or cut the forest) while away. It runs at the ordinary rate, online and offline, with the 8-hour cap. Moving takes over again.
   - Tools and packs are the supplier's own gear.
2. **Prospecting.**
   - Ore lives in hidden veins in the block grid, seeded from the world seed. The layer mix still sets the overall odds.
   - Flecks show on exposed faces next to ore.
   - The lamp sets how far signs can be read.
   - The prospector's tap: dull for plain rock, ring for ore, hollow for a pocket.
   - Blocks crack in stages.
   - Active play beats the routine's average (about ×1.3–1.5 from following veins), and is never required to progress.
3. **Railways, instead of routine slots.**
   - A shaft elevator lifts ore from any level to the surface staging.
   - A forest cart rolls logs from the cutting area to the forest door.
   - Both are bought upgrades that cut the walking back and forth.
4. **Deeper mining.**
   - Branching tunnels to chase veins.
   - Timbering every few blocks, which uses lumber, so the forest feeds the mine.
   - Hard pockets that need a better pick.
   - Rare finds (geodes, relics for quests).
   - A survey sketch per level.
   - An optional rhythm bonus on the swing.
   - Forest extras: choosing trees, felling direction, spotting herbs.
5. **Jobs from the castle.**
   - Job offerings come from a distant castle in the background.
   - Heroines and heroes come to the supplier to explain each mission and what they need, instead of the supplier posting jobs.
