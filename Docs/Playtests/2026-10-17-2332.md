## Could be better

1. **Design question: the gate can be walked round without the better axe on this world.** The band of old trees doesn't reach far enough, so the road's end is open on foot with the starting axe. Last time's investigation found the ends are usually closed by ordinary trees, and this world had a gap.
   - Reproduce: fresh Meadow's Edge camp (this run's world), walk from the Store counter up the road to the north rim.
   - Fix direction: decide whether the gate must be shut until the better axe. If so, make the old-tree band reach the hill or cliff on both sides. If not, treat the gap as fine.
2. **The button offers "Through the gate to River Bend" while moving on is still blocked.** At the road's end the game's own check says "Answer the castle's summons first: Old Shrine", but the button reads as if he can go now. I don't know what a tap shows, because the bot doesn't tap there. If it only says "not yet" after the tap, a first-time player may be confused. Fix direction: show the reason on the button (for example "Gate: answer the summons first") or grey it out.
3. **Two short stalls while walking (bot or game, unclear).** "46.8s stuck at (5.16, 3.27) heading for tree (12, 1): nothing visible" and "67.4s stuck at (-3.86, 7.57) heading for hill rock: nothing visible". Each was 1 snag, and he got there anyway (3.6 s and 8.1 s). Fix direction: if it shows up again, check for a small invisible blocker near those spots.
