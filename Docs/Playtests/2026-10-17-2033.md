# Playtest 2026-10-17 20:33

Bot run: 116 s of play at Meadow's Edge (fresh camp, stick and action button only). Test passed, 0 errors or warnings logged.

**Verdict:** Most of a first session plays through: exploring, chopping, digging, both deliveries and the Store visit worked. Two things stopped it. The bot never got the Forge's crafting panel, and it never reached the gate, so it couldn't move on to River Bend.

## Broken

1. **The bot couldn't get to the gate. It got stuck on the wagon.**
   - What happened: after the Store, the bot headed for the gate on the north rim. Its route planner found no route over open ground at all. It then walked straight toward the gate, ran into the wagon (its walls, counter rail and shafts), and was snagged 12 times in 30 s. It ended 12.5 units short. Timeline 85.4 s to 115.6 s. Screenshot: autoplay_10_gate.png (supplier pressed against the wagon's side wheel, button "Inspect Store Bell").
   - Why it may be the game: in autoplay_07_dug.png the sandy road north toward the gate signpost (top of the picture) has several trees standing right on it. If those trees are solid, the road is blocked. Last week's fix kept the exit cell itself clear, but maybe not the road leading to it.
   - Why it may be the bot: the bot also found "no route" to the Store counter (78.6 s) and still got there in 6.6 s by walking straight. Its map is coarse.
   - Reproduce: new game at Meadow's Edge, stand by the Store counter, then try to walk to the gate.
   - Fix direction: walk it by hand once. If it is blocked, keep the whole gate road clear of trees (not just the exit cell). If not, it's the bot's map.

2. **At the Forge, the button only offers "Inspect Forge Output".**
   - What happened: standing in front of the Forge (on the Blacksmith rug), the button said "Inspect Forge Output". Tapping it opened a "Forge Output" info card instead of the crafting panel. Timeline 77.8 s to 78.2 s. Screenshot: autoplay_08_forge_panel.png. The test suite's own blacksmith_working.png shows the same label with the supplier at the front of the rug.
   - Why it matters: a first-time player standing at the furnace with ore will expect to smelt. The output tray takes the button from the station they came to use.
   - Unsure: there may be another spot or the hammer button that opens crafting. If so, a new player wouldn't find it.
   - Fix direction: when standing near both the Forge and its output tray, let the Forge's own action win while the tray is empty.

## Could be better

3. **Trees in front hide the supplier.** After digging, the supplier is almost hidden behind pines in front of him. Only a hat brim and an arm show (autoplay_07_dug.png). The market stall does fade when he is behind it (autoplay_05_explore_SE.png), but trees don't. Fix direction: fade trees (or show an outline) when they cover the supplier.

4. **The debug status box covers the bottom-left of the play area.** "Bell rung · Session · Save gen" sits over the Apothecary/hut and its "0/6" counter (autoplay_03_explore_NW.png, autoplay_09_store.png, autoplay_10_gate.png). It also covers the supplier's legs (blacksmith_working.png) and the cliff vista (gate_drive.png). Fix direction: hide it in normal play, or move it into the settings menu.

5. **"Camp made at …" message spills out of its box.** On every new camp (level2_camp.png to level6_camp.png), the message runs across the "DEBUG SANDBOX · PHASE 9" banner. Its white text overflows the dark box onto the terrain above and below, so it is hard to read. Fix direction: let the box grow with the text, and place it below the banner.

6. **Levels 2 to 6 look almost the same where you arrive.** level2_camp.png to level6_camp.png show the same terraces, the same wagon spot and the same stall layout. Only the tree types change a little. Castle Approach (level6_camp.png) shows no castle and no hint of it from the arrival view. Fix direction: give each camp a landmark in view on arrival. Castle Approach is the natural place for the castle vista.

7. **Saving happens very often.** The save counter rises while the bot is only walking: 2 at the start, 11 at 14.6 s, 22 at 31.6 s, 81 at 116 s (about one save every 1.5 s). Fine in the editor, but it may cost battery or cause stutter on a phone. Fix direction: save on meaningful changes or on a slower timer.

8. **Two bubbles overlap over the Forge.** In blacksmith_working.png, the grey ore bubble (left) sits on top of the white ingot/coin pill, and the pill's right half is empty. Fix direction: offset or merge the bubbles, and size the pill to its contents.

9. **The carry count sits on the supplier and his pile.** "2/12" is drawn across his face and the logs (autoplay_06_chopped.png). In carry_views_eight.png, "6/12" collides with the top item of the pile when facing W, NW and SW. Fix direction: raise the count above the tallest pile item, or move it into the bag counter in the HUD only.

10. **Long two-line button labels.** "Inspect Apothecary Output" (autoplay_04_explore_SW.png) and "Inspect Display Table" (level2_camp.png) wrap onto two lines and fill the label edge to edge. Fix direction: shorter labels ("Apothecary tray") or a wider label.

11. **The supplier can walk into the red market stall.** In autoplay_05_explore_SE.png he stands inside its footprint while it fades. I'm not sure: it may be intended to be walk-through. Fix direction: check whether the stall should block.

12. **Frame time.** Median 16.7 ms. One worst frame of 190 ms in 6,860 frames, probably a load or first-use hitch (batch-mode numbers are relative only). Nothing else stands out.
