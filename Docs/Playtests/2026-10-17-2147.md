## Broken

1. **Holding the button at a standing tree pulled stumps instead of chopping it. The tree was never felled.**
   - What happened: the bot chopped two trees side by side, at 42 s and 47.5 s. It then walked to a third tree right next to them and faced it. The button said "Clear stump", not "Chop …". It held the button for 25 s. The carry went from 5 to 9, but the tree it faced was still standing at the end.
   - Why it matters: a player who walks up to a tree and holds the button expects to chop it. Here the stumps left by the trees he just felled come first. A human would probably turn a bit and try again, but it is the same kind of "wrong target" problem the rock-beside-a-tree fix solved.
   - Unsure: I can't see from the run which stump it picked, or whether it pulled several in a row. No screenshot was taken there.
   - Reproduce: fell two neighbouring trees, step to a third tree beside their stumps, face it and hold the button.
   - Fix direction: when the supplier faces a standing tree, prefer chopping it over a stump beside him. Or show "Clear stump" only when he faces the stump itself.

2. **The bot never reached the gate: it got stuck on the wagon for 30 s.**
   - What happened: after the Store counter (104.6 s), the bot found no route to the gate on its own map. It then walked straight north into the wagon's counter rail and walls. It snagged 12 times and ended 12.5 units short (autoplay_10_gate.png shows him still at the counter). Last time the same walk reached the gate in 20.8 s, with snags on the ox.
   - Game or bot: **mostly the bot**. It walks a straight line when its coarse map finds no route, and the wagon sits right in that line. Nothing about the wagon or the camp changed since last time. But the bot's map has now said "no route" from the counter to the gate in this run, and "no route" to the counter both times. So the way north past the wagon may be narrower than it looks. A quick human walk from the counter to the gate would settle it.
   - Because of this, I couldn't recheck the gate's wording while it's locked.
   - Fix direction: none for the game unless a human walk also feels cramped. If it does, widen the gap between the wagon, the ox and the hill.
