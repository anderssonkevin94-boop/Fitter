## Broken

1. **Digging beside a tree chops the tree instead of digging.**
   - What happened: the bot walked to a hill block at the edge of the forest and faced it from flat ground. The button said "Chop cedar log" instead of Dig. Holding it took 2.9 s and gave a cedar log, not rock or ore (timeline 68.1 s to 71.4 s; autoplay_07_dug.png shows the outlined tree just above the supplier and the button "Chop cedar log"). Last playtest, digging gave copper ore in 1.3 s.
   - Knock-on: the bot then carried the log to the Blacksmith's mat, which rightly refused it (87.7 s, "nothing was delivered"). That refusal is correct and not a fault in itself. But a player gets no visible hint of why the delivery failed (unsure: the bot only reads the carry count).
   - Why I think it's the game: last commit's change was meant to let a wall be dug even with a tree on top. Here the tree still takes the button while the supplier faces the rock. A player in this spot can't choose to dig.
   - Unsure: the tree may stand on a neighbouring cell rather than on the block the bot faced. Either way, the button picked the tree over the rock he was facing.
   - Reproduce: new game at Meadow's Edge, walk north-east to the hill edge in the forest (the block at map cell 20, 25), stand on the flat ground beside it, face the rock, read the button.
   - Fix direction: when the supplier faces a rock directly, let Dig win over a tree that is only nearby. Or show both choices.

2. **In the forest the supplier still disappears behind trees.**
   - What happened: the new fade works on a single tree (tree_fade.png), but it is faint. The supplier is still barely visible through it. In real play he walks into thick stands. At the dig spot (autoplay_07_dug.png) and at the gate (autoplay_10_gate.png) only his hat shows, behind 2 to 3 rows of solid pines. At the gate a first-time player couldn't tell where he is.
   - Reproduce: walk into the forest north of camp, or to the gate on the north rim.
   - Fix direction: fade every tree that covers the supplier (not just the nearest one), fade them further, or draw a silhouette of him through them.

## Could be better

3. **The gate says "Through the gate to River Bend", but the game won't let you go yet.** At the gate (112.3 s) the button offered the trip, but moving on was blocked with "Answer the castle's summons first: Old Shrine". That gating is by design (the journey's summons step). But a player isn't told about it on the way. The only hint is a red "1" badge on the map button, which appeared around 31 s (autoplay_05_explore_SE.png onward). The bot didn't tap, so I can't say what a tap at the gate shows. Fix direction: make the gate button say why it is closed ("Answer the summons first"), or point the badge at the summons.

4. **The debug status box still covers the bottom-left of the play area.** "Bell rung · Session · Save gen" covers the Apothecary hut and its "0/6" (autoplay_03_explore_NW.png, autoplay_09_store.png) and the supplier's legs (blacksmith_working.png). Unchanged from last time. Fix direction: hide it in normal play.

5. **The two bubbles over the furnace still overlap.** blacksmith_working.png: the grey ore bubble sits over the left end of the ingot/coin pill, and the pill's right half is still empty. Unchanged. Fix direction: offset the bubbles and size the pill to what it holds.

6. **The carry count still sits on the supplier's face.** "2/12" across his face and logs (autoplay_06_chopped.png). "1/12" across his chest by the wagon (autoplay_09_store.png). Unchanged. Fix direction: raise it above the pile, or show it only in the bag counter at the top.

7. **Forge panel: the last recipe's button is cut off at the bottom, and there's no close button in view.** autoplay_08_forge_panel.png: the Iron-bound Shield's craft button is cut off by the panel's bottom edge. Nothing shows that the list scrolls. The panel's header has no close (X) button. It may close by tapping outside; I can't tell from the screenshot. Fix direction: add a scroll hint or fade at the bottom, and a visible close button.

8. **Two-line button labels.** "Open Wagon counter" (autoplay_09_store.png) and "Through the gate to River Bend" (autoplay_10_gate.png) wrap onto two lines. "Open Apothecary" now fits on one line (it used to be "Inspect Apothecary Output"). Fix direction: shorter labels ("Wagon", "To River Bend").

9. **The supplier can still walk into the red market stall.** autoplay_05_explore_SE.png: he stands inside its footprint while it fades, same as last time. Not sure if this is meant to happen. Fix direction: decide whether the stall should block.

10. **The weapon stand's mannequin covers the supplier while he chops by camp.** autoplay_06_chopped.png: the mannequin and rack sit over his body, and only his hat and the log pile show. The stall fades for this; the weapon stand doesn't. Fix direction: give camp furniture the same fade.

11. **Frame time.** Median 16.7 ms. The worst frame was 165 ms out of 6,636 (last time 190 ms). This is probably a one-off load hitch, and batch-mode numbers are only relative.
